DEG Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

DEG is a social application that allows users to connect and share photos. This Privacy Policy explains how privacy is addressed when using the application.

1. Information and Content

Depending on the features used, users may provide account information, profile details, photos, captions, messages, and other content. Technical information may also be processed to operate, secure, and maintain the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide application features, display user content, support communication, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the service.

3. Sharing and Service Providers

Content that users choose to publish may be visible to other users. DEG may rely on third-party service providers to support application functionality. Their processing practices are governed by their applicable terms and policies.

4. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information. Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable policies. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

DEG is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable laws and the service's age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the service operator.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise manage your personal information. You may also stop using the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as DEG develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the DEG service operator through the official contact method provided with the application.
